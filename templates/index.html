ABOUT AIRSENSE

Environmental Analytics

AirSense uses a machine-learning classification model to analyze pollutant measurements and determine the corresponding air-quality category.

PM2.5

Fine particulate matter that can affect respiratory health.

PM10

Larger airborne particles commonly produced by dust and pollution.

NO₂

Nitrogen dioxide associated with vehicle and industrial emissions.

SO₂

Sulfur dioxide primarily associated with combustion processes.

CO

Carbon monoxide produced by incomplete combustion.

O₃

Ground-level ozone formed through atmospheric chemical reactions.
